How it works

  1. 1 · CreatePick what it is for and a date to finish by. It takes 20 seconds.
  2. 2 · ShareSend the link to the group. The preview shows how many juz are still free.
  3. 3 · ReadEach person taps a juz, reads it, and taps "I've read it". When all 30 are read, everyone sees the khatm is complete.

Questions

Does anyone need to install an app or make an account?

No. The link opens in any phone browser, including inside WhatsApp. People type their name once and tap a juz.

Can one person take more than one juz?

Yes. Tap another free juz to take it too.

What if someone takes a juz and cannot finish it?

They can tap "Give it back" so someone else can take it. Juz that were taken more than 3 days ago and are not read yet are marked, and the organiser can free them up.

My aunt is not on WhatsApp. Can she still take part?

Yes. The organiser can take a juz on her behalf, type her name, and mark it read when she tells you she has finished.

Can we do it again every month, or every Ramadan?

Yes. When all 30 juz are read, the organiser can start the next round on the same link, so the group keeps using one link.

Does the site show the Quran text?

No. Each juz links to Quran.com, where you can read or listen to it. You can also read from your own mushaf.

Who can see our khatm?

Only people who have the link. Khatm pages are not listed or shown in search engines. There are no ads and no tracking. A khatm is deleted after 400 days without activity, or straight away when the organiser deletes it.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Khatm Circle is free.
