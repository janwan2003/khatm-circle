Khatm Circle

Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026

What we store

For each khatm: its title, purpose, the optional name it is for, the optional message and finish date, and which juz are taken and read (or, for a group dhikr, the counts added). For each person who joins: the name they type and a random key that lets their phone act as them. We store only a scrambled (hashed) form of that key.

Your phone keeps your key in its browser storage so you stay signed in to the khatm. Nothing else is stored on your device, and there are no cookies.

What we do not do

No accounts, no email addresses, no phone numbers, no ads, no analytics or tracking scripts, and no selling or sharing of data. Khatm pages are marked so search engines do not list them.

Who can see a khatm

Anyone who has its link can see the title, the message and the names of the people reading. Only share the link with the group it is meant for.

How long we keep it

A khatm is deleted 400 days after its last activity, or at once when its organiser deletes it. To limit abuse we keep a counter per scrambled (hashed) network address for up to one hour.

Where it runs

Khatm Circle runs on Cloudflare (Pages, Functions and the D1 database). Cloudflare processes requests to deliver the site.

About

Khatm Circle is a free tool for group Quran khatms and dhikr. It does not host Quran text: each juz links to Quran.com, which has its own privacy policy.
