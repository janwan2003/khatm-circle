# khatm-circle

## Thesis

**Who:** Muslim families, friend groups, mosque and study circles, and colleagues who already
organise a group Quran completion (khatm/khatam) in WhatsApp: in Ramadan, for a deceased relative,
for someone who is ill, before an exam or wedding, or as a standing monthly circle. Also groups that
pool a dhikr or salawat count ("let's reach 100,000 together").

**What:** A no-login link that is the khatm. The organiser picks a purpose and an optional deadline,
and gets a link showing the 30 juz (parts) as 30 slots. Members open it from the group chat, type a
name, tap a juz to claim it, and tap "done" when they have read it. The page shows what is free,
claimed and finished, and nudges stale claims ("Juz 14 claimed 5 days ago: still reading?").
When all 30 are done, the group gets a completion card. A second mode is a shared counter for a
collective dhikr/salawat target where each person adds their count. A third is a repeating circle
that rotates juz each week or month. No Quran text is hosted at first; each juz links to an
established free reader.

**Needs others:** yes. The point of a group khatm is that 30 people each read one part; one person
cannot get the value alone, and the link has to go to the group to fill the slots.

**Viral moment:** (1) The link in the family or community WhatsApp group, whose preview shows live
state: "Khatm for Grandma Amina · 18 of 30 juz taken · 9 done". (2) The completion card ("30 of 30
juz completed in 6 days by 27 people") forwarded to other family groups. Every reader has a reason
to start their own khatm for their own family or mosque group, and "Start a khatm" is the natural
next action on the completion card.

**How often:** a yearly peak in Ramadan (expected to begin around 2027-02-08), plus khatms after
deaths, for the sick and for occasions, which happen year-round in large families. Standing circles
run monthly or weekly.

**Why now:** the practice is already common enough that fatwa sites answer questions about running
it on WhatsApp, and the organiser today copies and re-posts a numbered list by hand. The group apps
that exist need everyone to install an app.

## Market evidence

How people do it today (manual WhatsApp lists):
- Muftionline / IslamQA answers on [organising a Quraan khatam on WhatsApp](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/muftionline/114408/organizing-a-quraan-khatam-on-whatsapp/),
  [a monthly Quran khatam on WhatsApp](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/muftionline/111273/organising-a-monthly-quran-khatam-on-whatsapp/)
  and [WhatsApp Quraan groups](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/muftionline/132006/whatsapp-quraan-groups/).
  Rulings vary: one Hanafi answer discourages group khatms on social media in favour of individual
  recitation, and another calls a WhatsApp khatm for the deceased permissible (search summaries).
- Malaysia's federal mufti office issued [Irsyad al-Fatwa no. 471 on splitting Quran reading by juz](https://muftiwp.gov.my/en/artikel/irsyad-fatwa/irsyad-fatwa-umum-cat/4376-irsyad-al-fatwa-siri-ke-471-hukum-membahagikan-bacaan-al-quran-mengikut-juzuk)
  among WhatsApp members during the COVID-19 lockdowns. The ruling itself was not read.

Existing apps (all native installs; download counts not found):

  pool verses read. Reviews are mixed and mention bugs.
- [Sawab 30 Muslims](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1009404508): circles of 30 users, one juz each.
  One rating on the App Store; the [ArabNet coverage](https://arabnet.me/english/editorials/technology/apps/sawab-innovation-in-the-collective-reading-of-the-quran-) is from 2016.
- [Quran Khatmah](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6775534041) (weekly rotating 30-person circle),
  [KhatmApp](https://www.producthunt.com/products/khatmapp/reviews), [Always Read](https://apps.apple.com/sa/app/always-read/id1248209380),
  [Khitmah](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1509779816), [HaTeam](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hateam/id6756115835):
  small, too few ratings to show a summary.
- The large Quran apps are personal trackers. For example, [Khatmah](https://apple.co/2R5m3YF) claims
  18M+ users on its App Store listing but has no group khatm.
- Collective dhikr counters: [Zikr Together](https://apps.apple.com/ee/app/zikr-together/id6475817199)
  (global salawat count), [Dhikr Counter: Digital Tasbih](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1441547985)
  ("Dhikr Chain", real-time). All are install-only.

Checked 2026-11-21 (a research subagent opened the Google Play pages, en/US store, and read their "Downloads" band):
- Group-khatm apps on Play are small. [Quran Club](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.qc.andr.connect) has **100K+**
  installs and is the only one at scale. [Hatim Cüz Takip](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hatimtakip.hatim_cuz_takip) has 5K+;
  [Group Khatam Tracker](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.quraan.groupkhatam), [Khatm Group](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hatimgrubu.app)
  and [Hatim Dağıt](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hatimdagit.twa) have 1K+ each. KhatmApp, خِتام and "Hatim: Group Quran Khatm" have 100+.
  Sawab, Khitmah and Quran Khatmah are iOS-only. The personal planner [Khatmah](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.khatmah.android) has 10M+.
- Web tools: **hatimdagit.com** (Turkish, [open source](https://github.com/Yusuf-YENICERI/Hatim-Dagit), 26 stars) is a web hatim-sharing
  link. Its account and flow were not checked (the fetcher saw only its title). Its Android wrapper has 1K+ installs. Others do the job with
  a [paid $15 Sheets template](https://readquran.gumroad.com/l/khatm), public Google Sheets and Google Forms. No Arabic, Malay/Indonesian
  or Urdu web tool was found.
- Practice: [muftionline: "a person would take a parah"](https://muftionline.co.za/node/21891), [an admin "distributes Paras to members
  of the Whatsapp group"](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/themufti/152698/quran-khatams-through-whatsapp-groups/) (this one discourages
  social pressure, so the product never nags), and an Indonesian paper on WhatsApp khataman splitting each juz
  ([samawat](https://www.ejournal.badrussholeh.ac.id/index.php/samawat/article/download/433/388/846)).
- Terms: khatm/khatmah (Arabic), khatam with para/sipara (South Asia, South Africa), hatim with cüz (Turkish), khataman with juz/juzuk
  (Malay/Indonesian). Juz names follow [Wikipedia: Juz'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juz%27). `https://quran.com/juz/N` was fetched
  for N = 1, 2 and 30 (200) and 31 (404).
- Verdict: demand is real but the dedicated apps stay small because they need installs. The no-install link is the gap, and the
  Turkish market already has one web competitor.

Gap (2026-11-20 scout): I found no no-install web link that a WhatsApp organiser can drop into the group. Audience size
(about 1.9B Muslims) is general knowledge, not a sourced figure here, and I did not measure search
volume for "khatam group" or its Arabic, Urdu, Malay or Turkish equivalents.

## Our edge

- **Loop: needs others.** 30 slots that only other people can fill.
- **K estimate ≈ 1.9** = 40 people who see the link in family/community groups × 60% who claim a juz
  × 8% of readers who later start their own (for their own deceased relative, family group or
  mosque circle). Reason: one post reaches a whole WhatsApp group, claiming is one tap, and every
  family meets the occasions that start a khatm. The weakest factor is readers → creators; the
  completion card's "Start a khatm" button and the memorial use case are the levers.
- No install, no account, works in WhatsApp's in-app browser on cheap Android phones; light pages
  for slow connections.
- Live link preview (Open Graph) with "18 of 30 taken", which the manual list cannot do.
- Stale-claim nudges and a "release my juz" button fix the main failure of manual lists: one
  unfinished part blocks the khatm.
- Languages from day one: English, Arabic (RTL), Urdu, Malay/Indonesian, Turkish, French.
- Respectful design: no ads, no gamified leaderboard of who read most, a private-by-default option
  for memorial khatms, and no hosted Quran text at first (link to established readers).
- Risk: some scholars discourage social-media khatms. The product is neutral tooling for a practice
  people already do; content and wording should be checked with community members before launch.

## Status

**Live: https://pb-khatm-circle.pages.dev** (Pages deploy 8e11aad0). Product, support and privacy page:
https://janwan2003.github.io/khatm-circle/ . Search Console: site verified (META), added, and sitemap submitted on 2026-11-21
(`scripts/gsc.py`). English only so far.

What exists (vanilla JS, no build step):
- **Storage: one SQLite-backed Durable Object** (`KhatmDB`) in the Worker `pb-khatm-circle-db` (`worker/`, `workers_dev = false`,
  so it has no public URL). The Pages Functions bind to it. The account is at the free plan's **10 D1 databases** (9 studio
  ventures + the operator's `wegowhen`), so this venture has no D1. `worker/index.js` is a D1-style shim over `ctx.storage.sql`.
- Home: Quran khatm or group dhikr; 6 purposes (memorial, shifa = healing, Ramadan, occasion, circle, other) with an auto title;
  optional name it is for, finish-by chips (3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, 30 days) and a message; FAQ; "Your khatms on this phone".
- Khatm page `/k/<id>`: 30-juz grid (free / being read / read, your own juz ringed, a dot on juz taken more than 3 days ago and
  not read), progress bar, a juz sheet (Arabic opening words, start ayah, Quran.com link), take with only a name, "Your juz" card
  (Read / I've read it), give back, first-come claims ("Yusuf just took Juz 7"), "Invite the group" (Web Share, copy fallback),
  "Copy as a list" (the numbered WhatsApp list with ✅), personal link (`#k=`), live updates (polls a version every 10 s).
- Organiser: add a juz for someone not on the link (optionally already read), mark read for someone, free a juz, edit, delete,
  start round N+1 on the same link (round history kept).
- Completion card with "Start a khatm for your family" + a 1080×1080 result card (Web Share files, or press-and-hold/download).
- Group dhikr: shared counter to a target, tap counter kept in localStorage until added, +33/+100/+313/+1000/other, latest
  feed, undo my last, target-reached card.
- Link preview by `functions/k/[id].js` with live state ("18 of 30 juz taken · 9 read · 12 still free. Finish by …").
  Static og.png (not per khatm).
- Safety: tokens stored as SHA-256; rate limits per IP hash (create 20/h, join 60/h, writes 600/h); same-origin check on writes;
  CSP + security headers; 300 people per khatm; khatm pages `noindex`; deleted 400 days after the last activity.

Verified 2026-11-21:
- `node --test tests/unit/*.test.js`: 4/4 pass (juz table, purpose titles, preview text for quran/dhikr/complete, schema comments).
- `tests/e2e/run.mjs` in real Chromium (organiser desktop 1280 px, 4 readers on phone 390 px) **passes locally and on production**.
  Covered: create with validation, OG text before and after, join + take, Quran.com link, two juz per person, mark read, live update
  on the organiser page without reload, claim race, reader cannot act on others' juz, give back, organiser adds a juz for someone
  else (already read), frees a juz, marks read for someone, copy-as-list text, personal link on a new device (token stripped),
  completion card + OG "Khatm complete", result card image 1080 px, round 2 + history + OG "round 2", dhikr create / tap ×5 / +1,000
  with name / target reached / OG, delete → "gone" + API 404, privacy page, no sideways scroll on any screenshot, zero console errors.
  The test deletes its own khatms.
- Screenshots checked by eye (home desktop/phone, khatm phone, claim sheet, organiser desktop, completion, result card, dhikr).
  This found and fixed 3 bugs: a literal "null" under the page, the grid overflowing the phone width with long names, and a pill
  repeating the title.
- Live URL checks: `/`, `/privacy`, `/k/<id>`, og.png, icons, manifest, robots, sitemap 200; CSP and X-Frame-Options present.

Not covered yet: Arabic/Urdu/Malay/Turkish/French UI; real iOS Safari / Android and WhatsApp's in-app browser; how the preview renders
in WhatsApp; a per-khatm OG image; review of wording and juz names by practising users (the Arabic juz openings were written
from memory and match Wikipedia's transliterations, but nobody has proofread the Arabic); load beyond a handful of users. The single
Durable Object handles every khatm. That is fine at this scale; shard by khatm id if one object ever becomes the bottleneck.

## Next steps

1. **Languages (core for this audience):** add i18n with Arabic (RTL), Urdu (RTL), Malay/Indonesian and Turkish (hatim/cüz), then French.
   Use the per-community word in each (para/sipara, cüz, juzuk). Also localise the link preview text, chosen by the khatm's language.
2. Then set stage: launched. Run the quality pass on a real phone (operator ask in the inbox) and make a per-khatm OG image with
   the 30-tile grid (rendered in the Worker, or a cached PNG made with a canvas upload by the organiser).
3. Have 2–3 practising users proofread the juz names, the Arabic, and the memorial and shifa wording.
4. Reminder text the organiser can paste ("Juz 3, 14, 22 are still free. Who can take one?") and a "nudge" copy for stale juz.
5. Be fully localised by mid-January 2027, ahead of Ramadan (expected to start around 2027-02-08). Ramadan search pages
   ("ramadan khatm group", "khatam bersama") are the SEO angle.

## Monetization plan

None until the operator enables it (charter rule 4). This audience is sensitive to ads near
religious content. Later options are optional donations or a sponsor-free premium for mosques and
organisations (custom branding, many circles). Never put ads on the khatm page.
